Our story

A legacy of local craftsmanship

Wohletz Roofing & Construction started in 1994 with a simple goal: bring reliable, high-quality roofing and construction to Ponca City.

More than 30 years later, that hasn't changed. Our reputation was built one roof and one building at a time, on honesty, hard work, and a job done right the first time. Some of our customers called us decades ago, and they still call us today.

Today we specialize in two things: roofing for homes and businesses, and custom steel buildings for farms, shops and storage. Both are built for Oklahoma weather, and both get the same attention to detail.

Meet Justin Wohletz, Owner

Justin grew up in Ponca City and takes real pride in serving the community he calls home. He personally oversees every project, so every roof and every steel building meets his standards before we call it done.

When you call Wohletz, you're not getting passed around a call center. You're working with the guy whose name is on the sign.
