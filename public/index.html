Questions

Frequently asked questions

Still have a question? Give us a call at (580) 716-0113. You'll talk to a real person here in Ponca City.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Roof inspections and estimates are free, and so are steel building quotes. We'll come look, tell you what we see, and give you an honest price. No pressure.

Can you help with my insurance claim after a hail storm?

Yes. We inspect the roof, document the damage with photos, and work with your adjuster so nothing gets missed. If your roof doesn't need replacing, we'll tell you that too.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Wohletz Roofing & Construction is a licensed Oklahoma roofing contractor, license #80000665.

What kinds of roofs do you install?

Asphalt and composite shingles, metal roofing, and flat or low-slope commercial systems like membrane roofs. We handle homes, shops, barns, and commercial buildings.

What size steel buildings do you build?

Just about anything. Single-car garages, big multi-bay shops, equipment barns, covered RV and boat storage, and full self-storage facilities. If you can dream it, we can build it.

What areas do you serve?

Ponca City and the surrounding North Central Oklahoma area, including Blackwell, Tonkawa, Newkirk, Kaw City, McCord, Fairfax and Shidler.

Ready to get started?

Whether it's a leak that can't wait or a shop you've been dreaming about for years, we'd love to take a look. Estimates are always free.
