Storm & hail damage

After the storm, here's what to do

Oklahoma hail doesn't always leave obvious damage from the ground. Bruised shingles and cracked mats can turn into leaks months later, long after the claim window gets tight. A free inspection now gives you peace of mind.

  1. Stay safe and stay off the roof

    If water is coming in, move valuables and catch the drip. Leave the climbing to us.

  2. Call for a free inspection

    We check the shingles, vents, flashing, gutters and decking, and photograph everything we find.

  3. We work with your adjuster

    We'll meet your insurance adjuster and make sure all the damage gets seen. Then we make the repair or replacement.

Watch out for storm chasers

After every big hail storm, out-of-state crews show up with a truck, a clipboard and a great deal. Some do fine work. Many are gone by the time a problem shows up.

  • Ask for a local address and phone number
  • Check for an Oklahoma roofing license
  • Never sign over your insurance check before work is done
  • Get a written estimate

Roof repair questions

Still have a question? Give us a call at (580) 716-0113. You'll talk to a real person here in Ponca City.

How fast can you come look at my roof?

Call us at (580) 716-0113 and we'll get you on the schedule as quickly as we can. After a big storm things get busy, so it helps to call early.

Will my insurance cover hail damage?

Often it does, but every policy is different. We'll inspect the roof, document the damage with photos, and work with your adjuster. Your insurance company makes the final call on coverage.

Should I tarp my roof after a storm?

If water is actively coming in, a temporary tarp can prevent a lot of interior damage. Please don't climb on a wet or damaged roof yourself. Call us and we'll help.

Can you repair just part of a roof?

Yes. If the damage is limited and the rest of the roof is in good shape, a repair is usually the smart move. We'll tell you honestly which makes more sense.

Do you repair commercial roofs too?

Yes. We repair flat, low-slope, membrane and metal roofs on commercial and industrial buildings.
