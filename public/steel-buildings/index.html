Why steel?

The smart choice for strength and value

Wood rots. Termites eat it. Oklahoma wind loves to peel it apart. Steel just keeps going.

  • Built to lastRed iron frames and steel panels stand up to wind, hail and time.
  • Low maintenanceNo rot, no warping, no termites. Less upkeep year after year.
  • Flexible designWide clear spans and doors sized for whatever you're parking or working on.
  • Strong returnFor storage facilities and businesses, steel delivers long-term value.

Steel building questions

Steel building questions

Still have a question? Give us a call at (580) 716-0113. You'll talk to a real person here in Ponca City.

Do you handle the whole project?

Yes. We provide complete design and build services: site prep, concrete foundations, red iron framing, steel-clad walls and roof, and doors. One crew, one point of contact.

Can I choose the size and door layout?

Absolutely. Every building is built around how you'll use it, from building height and door sizes to walk-in doors, windows and auto bays.

Why choose steel over a wood pole barn?

Steel won't rot, warp or attract termites, it stands up to Oklahoma wind and weather, and it needs very little maintenance. For long-term value it's hard to beat.

Do you build commercial storage facilities?

Yes. We recently built Two Lakes Storage, a 50 unit boat and RV storage facility near Kaw Lake, and we build self-storage buildings of all sizes.

Can you add on to or retrofit an existing building?

Yes. We do expansions and retrofits on existing metal buildings.
