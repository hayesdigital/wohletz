Roof replacement

A new roof, done once and done right

When a roof is past its prime, patching it over and over just throws good money after bad. A full replacement fixes the problem for good, and it's one of the best ways to boost curb appeal.

We tear off the old roof down to the decking, replace any soft or rotted boards, then install new underlayment, flashing, vents and shingles. When we're done, we clean up like we were never there... except for the great-looking new roof.

Repairs & storm damage

Got a leak? Hail damage? Call us first.

A small leak today can mean rotted decking, ruined drywall and mold a few months from now. We fix leaks, flashing, missing shingles and storm damage quickly, and we'll help you through the insurance process if a storm caused it.

Roof repair & storm damage

Signs your roof needs attention

  • Water stains on ceilings or walls
  • Missing, curled or cracked shingles
  • Granules piling up in the gutters
  • Dents or bruises after a hail storm
  • Sagging spots or a soft feel underfoot
  • Daylight showing through the attic boards

Roofing questions

Roofing questions we hear a lot

Still have a question? Give us a call at (580) 716-0113. You'll talk to a real person here in Ponca City.

How do I know if I need a new roof or just a repair?

It depends on the age of the roof, how widespread the damage is, and whether the decking underneath is still solid. We'll inspect it, show you photos, and give you an honest recommendation. If a repair will do the job, that's what we'll tell you.

What roofing materials do you work with?

Asphalt and composite shingles, metal roofing, and flat or low-slope systems including membrane roofs for commercial buildings.

Do you handle insurance claims?

Yes. After hail or wind damage we document everything and work with your insurance adjuster to make sure the claim covers what it should.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes. We roof businesses, shops, warehouses and industrial buildings, and we pay close attention to safety and building codes.

Do you clean up after the job?

Always. Old shingles, nails and debris get hauled off, and we go over the yard before we leave.
